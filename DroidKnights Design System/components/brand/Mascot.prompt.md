Galaxy droid mascot. One `hero` + 9 poses. Copy `assets/mascot/*.png` into your project and point `base` at them.

```jsx
<Mascot pose="hero" base="assets/mascot" size={320} />
```

Keep the dark backdrop, don't recolor, one per view. Poses: hero, wave, point, cheer, laptop, flag, welcome, fly, portal, back.
